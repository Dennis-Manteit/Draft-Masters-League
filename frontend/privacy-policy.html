Draft Masters League Privacy Policy

Draft Masters League (“DML”, “we”, “us”) operates the DML website and competitions. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we use it, who we share it with, and how you can contact us about it.

This policy applies to the DML website and DML accounts. It should be read alongside our Terms and Conditions and the rules of any DML competition you join.

1. Information We Collect

1.1. Account Information. When you register, we collect the information needed to create and manage your account, such as your email address, username or display name, and an account identifier. If you sign in with Google, we may receive the basic account details you permit Google to provide.

1.2. Verification and Sign-In Information. We process information needed to verify your email address, sign you in, keep your session secure, and help you recover access to your account. Your password should be handled by the authentication service; DML officials do not need to know it.

1.3. League Information. We collect information needed to run the competitions you join, such as your team name, league membership, roster, selections, trades, claims, results, standings and relevant competition decisions.

1.4. NRL Fantasy Account Linking. If your NRL Fantasy account is linked to your DML account, we process the account identifiers and competition information needed to make that link and operate the DML competitions you join. We will describe the exact information transferred through the link before activating that feature.

1.5. Messages and Reports. If you email us, report misconduct, request help or dispute a decision, we collect the information you provide and any related correspondence needed to respond.

1.6. Technical Information. When you use the website, our hosting and security services may process information such as your IP address, device and browser details, sign-in events, and diagnostic records. We use this information to deliver the site, protect accounts and investigate technical problems.

1.7. Age Requirement. DML accounts are for people aged 18 or older. We do not intentionally collect account information from people under 18. If we discover that an underage person has registered, we will review and close the account and handle its information appropriately.

2. How We Use Information

2.1. Account and Competition Services. We use information to create and verify accounts, provide sign-in, link accounts where authorised, administer leagues, calculate or display competition information, and apply DML rules.

2.2. Safety and Security. We use relevant information to protect accounts, investigate suspected misuse or misconduct, enforce our Terms and Conditions, and respond to reports.

2.3. Support and Essential Messages. We use your contact details to respond to requests and send messages needed for your account, including sign-up verification. DML does not currently send promotional emails.

2.4. Improving the Service. We may use technical and competition information to diagnose faults and improve the reliability and usability of the website. We do not need to identify individual members when aggregated or de-identified information will serve that purpose.

3. Who Can See or Receive Information

3.1. Other Members. Other league members may see information necessary to run the competition, such as your display or team name, roster, matchups, scores, standings and permitted transactions. Your email address and sign-in details should not be displayed to other members as part of ordinary league use.

3.2. DML Officials. Authorised DML officials may access information needed for their defined duties, such as account support, league administration, safety reports and rule enforcement. Access should be limited to what each role needs.

3.3. Service Providers. DML uses service providers to operate the website, authentication, hosting and database. These providers may process information as needed to provide those services. DML currently uses Firebase services for the site’s sign-in and infrastructure. If you choose Google sign-in, Google also handles information under its own privacy terms.

3.4. NRL Fantasy Linking. When the NRL Fantasy linking feature is active, information needed to connect the accounts and run the relevant competition may be exchanged with the service used for that link. DML will explain the actual data exchanged before the feature is activated.

3.5. Safety and Legal Requests. We may disclose relevant information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or where reasonably necessary to address a serious safety concern. We do not make misconduct reports public.

4. Storage, Overseas Processing and Security

4.1. Storage and Overseas Processing. DML uses Firebase services to operate its website and accounts. Firebase Authentication processes account authentication information in the United States. Other DML information may be stored or processed in the locations used by the relevant Firebase service or other service provider. You can contact DML for information about the services used for your account.

4.2. Security Measures. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss and misuse. These include limiting administrative access and using the security controls provided by our hosting and authentication services. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

4.3. Retention. We keep information while your account is active and for as long as reasonably needed to administer competitions, resolve disputes, protect the service and meet legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or remove identifying details where reasonably possible.

5. Your Choices and Requests

5.1. Access and Correction. You can email draftmastersleague@gmail.com to ask what personal information DML holds about you or to request a correction. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before responding.

5.2. Account Closure and Disconnection. You can email us to request account closure or disconnection of a linked NRL Fantasy account. Disconnection may prevent participation in a competition that requires the link. We will explain any information that must be kept for an existing competition, dispute or legal obligation.

5.3. Privacy Complaints. If you believe we have mishandled your information, email draftmastersleague@gmail.com with the details. We will review the concern and respond within a reasonable time. If the Privacy Act applies to DML and you are dissatisfied with our response, you may be able to complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

6. Other Websites and Services

6.1. External Services. NRL Fantasy, Google and other services you choose to use have their own privacy policies. This DML policy covers DML’s handling of information; it does not replace those services’ policies.

6.2. External Links. The DML website may link to other sites. Their operators are responsible for their own privacy practices.

7. Changes to This Policy

7.1. Updates. We may update this policy when DML’s services or information practices change. We will publish the current version on the website and give reasonable notice of a significant change affecting members.

8. Contact

8.1. Privacy Contact. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, account closure, or complaints, email draftmastersleague@gmail.com.
