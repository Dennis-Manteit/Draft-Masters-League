# DML Draft Premiership Awards

**Status:** Award catalogue and past winners awaiting Operations confirmation. Nothing in this file declares an award winner.

The existing [Draft Premiership rulebook](../../../Rules/DML_Draft_Premiership.md) confirms that the regular-season ladder ranks tied teams by Points For; its top eight enter the championship finals. An equal Fantasy score in a knockout final is resolved in favour of the team higher on the final regular-season ladder. These rules can be used to verify finals results when the official fixture and ladder are available. They do not establish a full awards catalogue.

Operations must specify the names and eligibility criteria for any premiership, minor premiership, player, coach, weekly, or season awards; the scoring source and cut-off; ties and corrections; and who approves the result. Until then, the Honourary Roll must show an unavailable state rather than inferred winners.

For each approved award, retain season, competition, rule version, source fixture or statistic IDs, recipient display name, verification time, and approver. Respect recipient privacy and the current competition access policy.
